About PixPress

PixPress is an open-source, 100% safe, and completely offline image compressor and resizer created by ZiaMods Developers. Engineered for users who prioritize privacy and performance, PixPress reduces heavy high-resolution photos into compact file sizes with virtually zero visible quality degradation.

Unlike cloud-based image compressors that upload your personal gallery photos to remote servers, PixPress executes 100% of image decoding, resampling, and encoding directly on your Android device's hardware, ensuring total privacy.

Zero Internet Permissions The app manifest contains zero network permissions. No analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
Multithreaded Performance Compress single photos in milliseconds or batch compress entire folders seamlessly.
Modern WebP Converter Convert bulky PNGs and JPEGs into next-gen WebP images up to 80% smaller in size.
Ultra-Lightweight (271 KB) Minimal storage footprint, no bloatware, zero background battery drain.
